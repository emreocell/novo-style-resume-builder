# Exporting ATS text

Applicant tracking systems (ATS) often struggle with multi-column layouts, icons and photos. **Download ATS Text** produces a clean `.txt` with:

- Your name, title, summary and contact details (with full URLs)
- `WORK EXPERIENCE`, `SKILLS`, `COMMUNITY`, `EDUCATION` and `ACHIEVEMENTS` headings
- One line per role (`Title | Company | Dates | Location`) followed by `-` bullets

## When to use it

- Pasting into an application form's "resume text" field
- Portals that reject or mangle PDFs
- As a **backup**: you can re-import this file later to restore your data. See [Importing](import.md).
